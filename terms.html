Using Murshid

Murshid helps you find masjids and their Iqamah times. You can use it without an account. By creating an account or using Murshid’s social features, you agree to these terms.

Prayer times are a guide

Times come from each masjid’s published schedule and community reports, and they can be wrong or out of date. Please confirm with the masjid, especially for Jumu’ah, Ramadan and Eid. Murshid is provided “as is”, without any guarantee.

Zero tolerance for abuse

There is no place in Murshid for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not use a name, username or photo that is hateful, sexual, violent, harassing or that pretends to be someone else, and do not harass, threaten or spam anyone. This includes insulting anyone’s community, school of thought or beliefs.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block anyone from the Friends screen by tapping ••• next to their name. We review reports within 24 hours. If something breaks these rules we remove it, and we may suspend or delete the account of the person who posted it.

Your account

Keep your login safe and only create an account for yourself. You must be at least 13. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Delete account.

Suggestions you send

When you report a time, add a masjid or send feedback, you let us use it to improve Murshid’s listings. Please only send information you believe is true.
