Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

Murshid shows Iqamah times for masjids near you. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your data, there are no ads, and you can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Your location

With your permission, Murshid uses your location on your phone to sort masjids by distance and center the map. We do not store your location on our servers. The only exception is if you turn on “I’m there right now” when adding a missing masjid — then that one location is sent with your suggestion.

Preferences that stay on your phone

Which communities you choose to see, your Adhan calculation method and what matters to you in a masjid are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to our servers or shown to anyone.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. If you create one, we store your email address, the name, username, birthday and photo you add to your profile, your friends list, the prayers you RSVP to, check-ins, favorite masjids and the days you log attending jama’ah. Other signed-in users can see your name, username and photo so friends can find you; your birthday stays private. Friends you accept can also see the prayers you RSVP to. Check-ins are only ever shown to others as totals (for example, “12 people checked in”), never with your name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us.

Things you send us

When you send feedback, report a wrong time, suggest a missing masjid or report another user, we store what you send. A report also keeps a copy of the reported person’s name and photo so we can review it; the person you report is never told who reported them. If you block someone, we store that so we can keep them away from you. If you add an email address to feedback, we only use it to reply to you. If you are signed in, these are linked to your account.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your data and we do not use advertising or tracking tools. Your data is stored by Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. Maps are shown by Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android), and tapping Directions opens your maps app. We may share information if the law requires it.

Where masjid information comes from

Masjid listings come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License), public IRS nonprofit records located with the U.S. Census Geocoder, and suggestions from the community. Prayer times come from each masjid’s own published schedule, which is credited on each masjid.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data until you delete your account. You can delete it anytime in Settings > Delete account; this permanently removes your profile, photo, friends, blocks, RSVPs, check-ins, favorites and attendance. Feedback and suggestions you sent are kept without your name so we can keep improving the app. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for deletion.

Children

Murshid is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
