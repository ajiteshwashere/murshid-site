Murshid support

Murshid shows live Iqamah times and countdowns for masjids across Texas. Here’s how to get help.

A prayer time looks wrong

Open the masjid in Murshid and tap “Update”. Tell us the right time and we’ll fix it. Times come from each masjid’s own schedule and refresh twice a week.

A masjid is missing

On the Map, tap “Add a masjid”. Add its name and address (or tap “I’m there right now”) and we’ll add it after a quick check.

Someone is being abusive

Open Friends, tap ••• next to their name and choose Report or Block. We review every report within 24 hours. Blocked people can’t find you or send you requests.

Delete my account

In the app go to Settings › Delete account. Everything tied to your account is erased right away. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

Anything else

Use Settings › Send feedback in the app, or email us. We usually reply within a few days.
